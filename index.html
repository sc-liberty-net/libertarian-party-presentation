The gaps

Where The Bylaws Go Quiet

“Cause” Is Never Defined

The LNC can revoke a state affiliate “for cause” by a 3/4 vote. What counts as cause is left open.

Article 5.6

No Notice Before The Vote

The motion must name the cause. Nothing requires warning the affiliate or hearing from it first.

Article 5.6

The State Carries The Burden

On appeal, the affiliate must prove the LNC wrong. If no ruling comes within 30 days, the revocation stands.

Article 5.6

Most Disputes Have No Forum

The Judicial Committee hears seven listed matters, and can refuse anything tied up in outside litigation.

Article 8.2 · JC Rule 2.8(h)

Part 1 · Checks and balances

Put A Second Key On The Big Powers

Council Of State Chairs

A standing body that must ratify revocations, nominee suspensions and the LNC’s picks for empty seats.

Enumerated Powers

The LNC holds only the powers the bylaws list. The rest stays with the states and the convention.

No Self-Appointment

The LNC stops filling its own empty seats. Seat the next-highest finisher, or require Council confirmation.

States Pick The Rule-Writers

Affiliates, not the LNC, choose the Bylaws, Credentials and Platform committees.

A Neutral Convention Chair

Delegates elect a presiding officer who is neither a party officer nor a candidate.

Why It Works

A faction that controls the LNC would also need most of the states to agree.

How the pieces fit

One Party, Separated Powers

The convention · two chambers, both must agree

States Chamber

LNC

Acts. Runs the party between conventions.

Reviews. Appeals, with the burden on whoever acted.

Part 3 · Representation

Give States & Members A Voice

Chamber 1

States

Two delegates per state, chosen by those State Committee Officers who compr both the previous and newly elected State Committees party. Every state counts equally, the way the Senate works.

Chamber 2

State Delegates

Those delegates of the State Convention elected to represent the affiliate at the National Convention.

One per state

People’s Champion

Elected by all official members of the affiliate State Party regardless of those members' delegate status to prior Conventions. 


The PC can petition the Judicial Committee, force an advisory member vote, and delay a revocation.

Revocations and changes to the Bylaws or the Party Platform need both chambers to agree.

The plan
